Save an Apple Pay expense

iOS 26

1. Wallet

Open Settings → Wallet & Apple Pay. Under Allow Wallet & Apple Pay to Access, turn on Live Activities and Mobile Data.

2. Automation

Open Shortcuts and go to the Automation tab. Tap + at the top right. The first time, the button in the middle says New Automation.

3. Wallet trigger

Choose Wallet — “When I tap a Wallet Card or Pass”.

4. Cards and categories

Select the cards you pay with. Leave the categories checked. Tap Next.

5. Run immediately

Choose Run Immediately, not Run After Confirmation. Leave Notify When Run off. Tap Next.

Run After Confirmation only posts a small system notice. The Clio card does not show.

6. A new shortcut

Tap the gray Create New Shortcut card at the top left. Do not pick a shortcut from the list below.

7. Clio’s action

In the search at the bottom, type Clio. Tap Save Wallet Expense.

You should see two blocks: “Receive transaction as input” and Save Wallet Expense. Amount and Merchant are still empty.

8. Amount

Tap the Amount field. Above the keyboard, tap Select Variable.

Tap the blue Shortcut Input chip under “Receive transaction as input”.

A chip that only says Shortcut Input is not finished. That passes the whole transaction, and Shortcuts fails before Clio can read the amount.

Tap that chip again. The sheet says Type: Transaction. Do not leave the checkmark on Transaction. Scroll and tap Amount.

The field then shows a blue Amount chip, not Shortcut Input.

9. Merchant

Tap Merchant and repeat the same path. On the Transaction sheet, choose Merchant, not Transaction.

10. Save

Tap the blue checkmark at the top. The automation appears in the list. The next payment with that card asks you to save the expense in Clio.
